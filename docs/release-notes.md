# Cilevi release notes

Every version of Cilevi that reached the Microsoft Store, newest first. The Store installs each one by itself, so you do not need to download anything from here. To see which version you have, open **Menu** in the Studio and choose **About Cilevi**.

Cilevi for Mac is waiting for Apple's review. Its notes will start here once it is in the Mac App Store.

## Cilevi 2.4.1, 29 October 2026

- Cilevi now speaks Spanish. Choose **Language** in the recorder's settings menu or in the Studio's menu to switch between English and Spanish.
- The first time the recorder opens, if your connection is in a country where Spanish is widely spoken, Cilevi asks whether you would like Spanish. It never changes the language without your yes, and it does not ask again.
- Your recordings, projects and caption language are not affected by the interface language.
- Licence keys bought on cilevi.com are now sold through Paddle, so you can buy one from nearly every country. Your sales tax or VAT is shown before you pay.
- **Already paid?** in the License window now finds a key bought on the website: confirm the email address you paid with and the key is filled in for you.

## Cilevi 2.3.0, 26 October 2026

- Tell Cilevi what you are recording. When it opens it asks whether this is a tutorial or product demo, or a game. A game recording uses the Modern capture method and turns every automatic effect off, so clicks and shots do not make the picture zoom.
- One switch in the settings menu turns all automatic effects on or off: automatic zooms, pointer effects, and click and key sounds. Your own pencil drawing is never affected.
- **Stop** always finishes, even when the window you are recording has stopped responding.

## Cilevi 2.2.2, 26 October 2026

- Smoother recordings at 60 frames a second, especially of games and other busy screens. Far fewer repeated frames when the graphics card is working hard.
- If the graphics card stalls for a moment during a take, the recording holds the last picture instead of losing it.

## Cilevi 2.2.1, 26 October 2026

- Fixes a pause of several seconds that could freeze the picture partway through a recording on some computers.

## Cilevi 2.2.0, 25 October 2026

- A welcome offer: after your first recording, Cilevi offers a licence at 20 percent off for 24 hours, once.
- Cilevi records one display by default, the one the recorder toolbar is on. If you pick a display yourself, it is remembered, and the first recording tells you what it is about to capture.
- Fullscreen recording stays correctly fitted when a display changes resolution, for example when a game starts, and the pointer and clicks stay lined up with the picture.
- Recordings finish more safely, and the message is clearer when a take cannot finish.
- You choose the export settings before saving a new project, with separate steps for the editable project and the finished MP4 or GIF.
- You can delete a take you do not want, together with its companion files, after a separate confirmation. **Move to Recycle Bin** is selected by default.
- After **Stop**, the finished recording is shown in File Explorer.

## Cilevi 2.1.0, 21 October 2026

- Record games and 3D apps. A new capture method setting (Automatic, Modern or Classic) records windows that used to come out black.
- Draw on the screen while you record. Press F8 for the pencil and right click to wipe. The drawing is never burned into the video: it arrives in the Studio as its own layer that you can move, resize, fade or delete.
- Change any keyboard shortcut from one **Keyboard shortcuts** window, in the recorder and in the Studio.
- **Show Cilevi in recordings**: a switch for filming Cilevi itself, for tutorials and reviews.

## Cilevi 2.0.4, 18 October 2026

The first version of Cilevi in the Microsoft Store for everyone.

- Record a display, a window or a part of the screen, with your microphone, your camera and the computer's sound.
- The Studio opens with the take already edited: zooms that follow your clicks and stay in while you type, a smooth pointer, a background and a rounded frame.
- Captions written on your own computer in 99 languages. Edit the video by deleting words from the transcript, and remove filler words.
- Trim, split, speed up, freeze a frame, join several recordings, and add titles, arrows, blur, music and a voiceover.
- Export an MP4 or a GIF, wide or vertical.
- Free for seven days with every feature and no watermark, then a licence key for 50 dollars, once.
