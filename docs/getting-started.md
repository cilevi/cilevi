# Getting started with Cilevi

This takes you from installing Cilevi to your first exported video. Try it once with something unimportant; it is the quickest way to see what Cilevi does for you.

## 1. Install it

Get Cilevi from the [Microsoft Store](https://apps.microsoft.com/detail/9NB07ZW49F3D) and start it from the Start menu. You need Windows 10 or 11, 64 bit.

The first time, Cilevi shows its privacy policy, terms of use and refund policy. Press **I agree** to carry on. It then offers to register an email address. You can skip that: every feature works the same without it. Registering only means a problem report can be answered.

Cilevi also asks what you are recording: a tutorial or product demo, or a game. For a game it switches the automatic zooms and effects off. You can change this later in the settings menu.

The recorder toolbar then appears at the bottom of your main screen, and a Cilevi icon sits in the system tray.

## 2. Choose what to record

Click the pill on the toolbar that shows a size. Pick a display, **Window...** to point at one window, or **Region...** to drag a box. The microphone and camera pills on the right switch those on. Both are off until you turn them on.

## 3. Record

Press the red button, or **F9** from anywhere. A three second countdown runs first. Then do something for twenty or thirty seconds: click a few things, scroll, type into a box. The clicks are what the zooms are built from.

Press **F9** again to stop. Cilevi asks what to do with the take. Choose **Creator Studio**. (**Export Now** keeps the file exactly as recorded, with no editing.)

## 4. Look at what Cilevi already did

The Studio opens with your take on the timeline. Press play. The picture zooms in around each click and stays in while you type, the pointer moves smoothly, and the recording sits on a background in a rounded frame. You did none of that by hand.

Everything can be changed: drag a zoom on the timeline, pick another background in the panel on the right, or add captions from the **Captions** tab. Captions are written on your computer; the first time, Cilevi downloads a speech model once.

## 5. Export

Press the blue **Export** button at the top right, or **Ctrl+E**. Cilevi first asks where to save the project, so you can come back to it. Leave the export settings as they are, press **Export...**, choose where to save the video, and let it run.

## 6. After seven days

Cilevi is complete for seven days, with no watermark. To keep using it, buy a licence key on [cilevi.com/buy](https://cilevi.com/buy) for 50 dollars, once. The key arrives by email. In Cilevi, open the gear (settings) on the recording bar, or **Menu** in the Studio, choose **License...**, paste the key under **Or enter a licence key**, and press **Activate**.
