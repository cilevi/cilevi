# Getting help with Cilevi

## Where to ask

- **A bug:** [open an issue](https://github.com/cilevi/cilevi/issues/new/choose) here. Issues are public, so leave out anything private.
- **A question or an idea:** [Discussions](https://github.com/cilevi/cilevi/discussions), or the [feedback board](https://cilevi.com/feedback) on cilevi.com, where other people can vote.
- **Privately, to a person:** the [contact page](https://cilevi.com/contact) or support@cilevi.com. Use this for anything about your purchase or licence key.
- **From inside the app:** **Report a problem** sends us your version and the app's own log along with your message (see below).

## Finding your version number

In the Studio, open **Menu** and choose **About Cilevi**. On the recorder, the same window opens from the gear (settings) menu with **Privacy & Terms...**, or from the tray icon. Near the top it says **Version** followed by the number and the date it was built. Copy that whole line into your report.

## What a good bug report holds

1. **Your Cilevi version**, the whole line from About Cilevi.
2. **Your Windows version**, for example Windows 11 24H2. You find it in Settings, System, About.
3. **What you did, step by step**, in enough detail that somebody else could do the same thing and see the same problem.
4. **What you expected to happen.**
5. **What happened instead**, with the exact words of any message, and a screenshot if it helps.
6. **Whether it happens every time**, or only sometimes.

"It does not work" cannot be looked into. "I pressed Export with a 9:16 canvas and a camera on, and the camera was cut off at the top" can often be fixed the same day.

## Report a problem, inside the app

**Support** on the Studio's top bar opens the **Report a problem** window. The same window is on the recorder's settings menu, on the tray icon's menu, and in About Cilevi. It fills in your version, Windows version, settings and the end of the app's log, and you can read and delete any line before sending. It never sends anything you recorded: no video, no sound, no picture. The log does name the files and folders you recorded to, and the window says so.

If Cilevi will not start at all, write to support@cilevi.com with your Windows version and what you saw.

## Before you report

- Captions need a processor with AVX2. Without it, the Captions tab says so, and the Windows speech recogniser or hand typed captions still work.
- On the N and KN editions of Windows, Cilevi needs the Media Feature Pack. It tells you, and offers to open the right page in Windows Settings.
- A missing camera or microphone: if it does not work in the Windows camera or sound settings, it will not show in Cilevi either.

More in the [troubleshooting part of the guide](https://cilevi.com/guide).
